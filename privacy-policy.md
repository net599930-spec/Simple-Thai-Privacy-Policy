# Privacy Policy

**Simple Thai Phrasebook**
Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Simple Thai Phrasebook app ("the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. If you have any questions, contact me at **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- You can use the whole app **without an account**. In that case everything stays on your phone and nothing about you is sent to me.
- If you **choose to log in**, your email address and your learning progress are stored on a server so they can sync between your devices.
- There are **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
- You can **delete your account and all its data** at any time from inside the app.

## 1. Using the app without an account

When you use the app as a guest, your progress, bookmarks, spelling times and settings are saved **only on your device**. The app downloads the phrasebook content (words, sentences and lessons) from my server, but this download contains nothing about you.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this guest progress is gone.

## 2. Using the app with an account

Logging in is optional. You can log in with Google or with an email address and password. When you do, the following is stored:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address, plus the name your Google account provides if you log in with Google | To create your account and let you log in |
| Learning progress: how well you know each word and sentence, review counts and dates, bookmarks, completed units, and spelling practice results | To save your progress and sync it between your devices |
| Your custom knowledge-level colours | To keep your colour choices on all your devices |

When you first log in, any progress you made as a guest on that device is added to your account.

Display settings such as text size, font and dark mode stay on your device and are not uploaded.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** this data is processed to provide the service you asked for by creating an account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## 3. Services the app uses

- **Supabase** (supabase.com) hosts the database and handles logins. Your account data and progress are stored there.
- **Google Sign-In** is used only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google confirms who you are and shares your email address and basic profile with the app. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Text-to-speech** uses the speech engine already on your phone to read Thai aloud. The app does not send your text anywhere for this.

These providers process data only to run the app and under their own privacy and security terms.

## 4. What I don't do

- No advertising
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No selling or renting of your data
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files

## 5. How long data is kept

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and its progress are deleted from the server.

## 6. Deleting your account

**In the app:** open the **More** tab → **Account** → **Delete account**, and confirm. This permanently deletes:

- your account (email address and login)
- all synced progress, bookmarks, spelling results and colour settings

It also removes this data from the device you delete it on. This cannot be undone.

**Without the app:** email **[YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]** from the address linked to your account and ask for your account to be deleted. I'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## 7. Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. In Slovenia, that is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si). To use any of these rights, contact me at the email above.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can only read and change its own progress.

## 9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact me and I'll delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
